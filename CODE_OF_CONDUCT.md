# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the Web Terminal project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standard

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its Code of Conduct. The full text — including the detailed standards of
acceptable and unacceptable behavior, enforcement responsibilities, scope, and
enforcement guidelines — is published at:

> https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, give and
gracefully accept feedback, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, personal attacks, and other conduct prohibited by the Contributor
Covenant are not tolerated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository,
issues, pull requests, and discussions — and also applies when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement & Reporting

This is a single-maintainer project. Instances of abusive, harassing, or
otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainer
through GitHub:

> https://github.com/Adiel-Sharabi

For **security vulnerabilities**, do not use this channel — follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
instead.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, on a
best-effort basis. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and
security of the reporter of any incident.
